# 新研究建模「幻觉滚雪球」在多智能体 LLM 流水线中的传播

[p002]
A new ICML 2026 workshop paper formalizes the "hallucination snowball effect" in multi-agent LLM pipelines, showing that GPT-4o detection of injected errors drops from 72.0% at Stage 1 to 50.9% at Stage 4, and that boundary-gate verification reduces hallucination survival from 58.4% to 16.2% while end-of-pipeline checking is nearly useless.
