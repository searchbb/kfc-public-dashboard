**SAN FRANCISCO, September 11, 2026 —** Every company is racing to turn AI into real business results. But the hardest part is no longer getting AI to answer a question. It’s getting AI to do the work that matters across every business function.

[p004]

That experience has made one thing clear: putting agents to work requires more than intelligence. Agents need to understand the job, work across the business, and keep moving toward a goal as the work gets more complex.

[p005]
Today, Salesforce is introducing a new portfolio of job-ready agents built to take on high-value work across sales, service, commerce, employee experience, and the back office. Connected to Customer 360, these agents can work with the customer context and business processes companies already have in Salesforce. These agents come ready for the job and are powered by new [Agentforce](https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/) technology that enables them to pursue goals over time, learn new skills, work with other agents, and continuously improve.

**Take on work that spans days and weeks**

**Take on work that spans days and weeks**

[p022]
Some of the most valuable work in a business can’t be completed in a single conversation. A seller doesn’t just research an account once. Winning an opportunity can require weeks of outreach, new information, changing priorities, and decisions along the way.

[p023]
That’s why Salesforce built a new long-horizon runtime for Agentforce, enabling agents to pursue goals across days and weeks instead of completing only a task or interaction.

[p024]
With the new runtime, a seller might ask Hunter to rescue their at-risk deals before the end of the quarter. Hunter can turn that objective into a measurable goal, build a plan, and begin working toward it — determining the tasks to complete, the tools and context required, and the guardrails that define when it can act autonomously and when seller approval is required.

[p025]
As the work unfolds, Hunter can incorporate new information and adjust the plan while keeping the seller in control. Underneath, three capabilities make that possible:e make that dependable:

[p026]
- **Memory**preserves context and progress across sessions, so work doesn’t stop when the interaction ends.
- **Durable execution**keeps plans running over time and allows an agent to resume or course-correct as circumstances change.
- **Dynamic steering**adapts an agent’s behavior based on an individual user’s feedback and direction.

[p027]
Hunter is the first agent to run on the long-horizon runtime. Over time, more agents across the portfolio will run on it, and customers will be able to build long-horizon agents of their own with Agentforce.
