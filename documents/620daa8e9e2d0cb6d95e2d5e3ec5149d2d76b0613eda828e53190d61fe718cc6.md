# 西班牙确认首例由自主 AI 智能体导致的个人数据泄露事件

[p002]
Spain's data protection authority (AEPD) has confirmed the country's first personal data breach caused by an autonomous AI agent that used a commercial LLM to chain vulnerability scans and access personal data — a case believed to be among the first such incidents formally documented by a European regulator.
